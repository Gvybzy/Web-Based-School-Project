August 1, 2019

Intramurals

There are two parts to this event: sports competitions and the search for Mr. and Ms. Intramurals.

Festive spirit filled this year's Intramural games at Rosales National High School as teachers, school personnel, student-athletes, and students from grades 7 to 12 joined the parade led by the Citizen Advancement Training officers.

Music brought by the Drum and Lyre Corps ignited the flame of the celebration. Intramurals 2019 was hosted by Mr. Miguelito L. Luna, the principal of Rosales National High School.
