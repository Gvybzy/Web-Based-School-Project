Student conduct

School Rules and Regulations

General conduct guidelines that keep RNHS a safe, orderly, and respectful learning environment for every student.

  • There is only one gate for entrance and exit. Students are required to enter and leave the school only through this gate. Climbing and/or jumping over the perimeter fence is strictly prohibited.
  • Students should arrive at school in time for the Flag Ceremony on Mondays, and before the start of classes on other weekdays.
  • Students should come to school in their proper uniform Mondays through Fridays unless otherwise stated, and should wear a valid, signed ID bearing their picture at all times.
  • Wearing the complete prescribed uniform, while voluntary in some cases, is strongly encouraged for identification and student safety.
  • The prescribed uniform for Junior High School is a white blouse and navy blue skirt with black closed shoes and white socks (girls), and a white polo with black or blue pants, black shoes, and white socks (boys).
  • The prescribed uniform for Senior High School is a white blouse with a grey collar and pleated skirt (girls), and a white polo with a grey collar and grey or black pants (boys). All students are expected to follow the dress code policy.
  • Students are discouraged from wearing tight-fitting pants or blouses, mini skirts, shorts, plunging necklines, crop tops, sando tops, ripped pants, or sleepwear. Clothing with indecent markings is not allowed, and cross-dressing is strictly prohibited.

Discipline framework

Different Kinds of Offenses

Committing any of the offenses below — whether against another person, property, public morals, or security and public order — is subject to disciplinary action and corresponding sanctions.

01 Offense Against Other Persons

Offenses committed against a fellow student or a member of school personnel.

02 Offense Against Property

Offenses that damage, steal, or destroy the property of the school, a fellow student, or school personnel.

03 Offense Against Public Morals

Offenses that cause harm to another person's moral standing or reputation.

04 Offense Against Security and Public Order

Offenses that undermine the security and public order of the school campus.
