Tree Planting Activity

Simultaneous Tree Planting Activity in Support of Project DepEd's 236,000 Trees Christmas Gift for the Children.

Tree planting in Rosales National High School (RNHS) is an essential initiative aimed at promoting environmental sustainability and fostering a sense of responsibility among students. Through organized tree-planting activities, the school encourages students and faculty members to actively participate in greening the campus, enhancing the local ecosystem, and combating climate change.

These efforts not only help improve air quality and biodiversity but also serve as an educational tool, teaching students about the importance of trees in maintaining ecological balance. The project is a collaborative endeavor, with the goal of creating a greener, healthier, and more beautiful environment for the entire school community.
