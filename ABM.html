Academic Track
(ABM)


Seeks to prepare the young business leaders of tomorrow. ABM strand paves the way for business-related college degrees. It teaches the basic concepts of financial management, accounting, and corporate operations. The programs under this strand aims to instill crucial skills that one needs to perform well in their future careers and businesses.

What is ABM Strand?


The Accountancy, Business, and Management (ABM) strand is designed for students who aspire to pursue careers in business, finance, and entrepreneurship. It equips learners with essential skills in financial management, marketing, and business operations, preparing them for real-world corporate and entrepreneurial challenges. Through subjects like economics, business ethics, and accounting, students develop analytical and decision-making skills crucial for business success. The ABM strand also fosters leadership, communication, and strategic thinking, making it an excellent choice for future business leaders, accountants, and entrepreneurs. By taking this track, students gain a solid foundation for higher education and career opportunities in various business-related fields.
