Simultaneous Tree Planting Activity

An initiative to promote environmental preservation and instill environmental responsibility in Filipino children.

Together, RNHS made this event possible with the RNHS-SHS SDRRMC, Yes-O, and the Math Club, all contributing to a school-wide effort to plant trees in support of Project DepEd's 236,000 Trees Christmas gift for the children.

The activity was carried out with the guidance and support of Principal IV, Ma'am Rowena Lea G. Moreno.
