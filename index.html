Who we are

Welcome to Rosales National High School

RNHS is a public secondary educational institution located in Rosales, Pangasinan, established to provide quality and affordable education to its residents. We implement the K to 12 basic education program, offering both junior and senior high school in accordance with the Department of Education (DepEd).

Our dedicated faculty and staff work together to help students excel academically while developing strong values, leadership skills, and a sense of responsibility.
