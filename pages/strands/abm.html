What is ABM?

Accountancy, Business, and Management

The Accountancy, Business, and Management (ABM) strand is designed for students who aspire to careers in business, finance, and entrepreneurship. It equips learners with essential skills in financial management, marketing, and business operations, preparing them for real-world corporate and entrepreneurial challenges.

Curriculum

What Students Learn

  • Economics and business ethics
  • Financial accounting and management
  • Marketing and business operations
  • Entrepreneurship fundamentals
Beyond the classroom

Skills Developed

  • Analytical decision-making
  • Leadership
  • Communication
  • Strategic thinking
Where it can lead

Possible Pathways

Graduates often pursue college programs in accountancy, business administration, finance, marketing, or entrepreneurship — and go on to careers as accountants, business owners, and corporate professionals.

These are common directions graduates pursue and are not a guarantee of admission, licensure, or employment.
