What is GAS?

General Academic Strand

The General Academic Strand (GAS) is ideal for students with diverse interests who are still exploring their career paths. It provides a well-rounded education by drawing on subjects from humanities, business, science, and the social sciences.

Curriculum

What Students Learn

  • Humanities and social sciences
  • Introductory business subjects
  • General science
  • Applied economics and electives
Beyond the classroom

Skills Developed

  • Critical thinking
  • Problem-solving
  • Communication
  • Adaptability
Where it can lead

Possible Pathways

GAS keeps options open, preparing students for college programs such as education, liberal arts, hospitality management, and more.

These are common directions graduates pursue and are not a guarantee of admission, licensure, or employment.
