Academic Track
(GAS)


While the other strands are career-specific, the General Academic Strand is great for students who are still undecided on which track to take. You can choose electives from the different academic strands under this track.


These subjects include Humanities, Social Sciences, Applied Economics, Organization and Management, and Disaster Preparedness.

What is GAS Strand?


The GAS (General Academic Strand) strand in our Academic Track offers a flexible and well-rounded educational experience for students exploring diverse fields. It provides a comprehensive foundation for those who have not yet decided on a specific career path, offering a broad range of knowledge and skills applicable to various fields of study in higher education.


The General Academic Strand (GAS) is ideal for students who have diverse interests and are still exploring their career paths. This strand provides a well-rounded education by incorporating subjects from different academic disciplines, including humanities, business, science, and social sciences. GAS allows students to develop critical thinking, problem-solving, and communication skills that are applicable to various fields. It is a flexible track that prepares learners for college programs such as education, liberal arts, hospitality management, and more. With its broad curriculum, GAS equips students with a strong foundation for future studies and career opportunities, making it a great choice for those who want to keep their options open.
