Intramurals

Intramurals 2019 was hosted by Mr. Miguelito L. Luna, the principal of Rosales National High School.

Intramurals 2019 at RNHS was a highly anticipated event that brought together students, teachers, and staff in a vibrant celebration of sportsmanship, teamwork, and school spirit. The event featured a variety of athletic competitions, including basketball, volleyball, badminton, and track and field, where students showcased their skills and determination.

Cheer dance performances and fun games added excitement to the festivities, fostering camaraderie among different teams. The friendly yet competitive atmosphere encouraged students to push their limits while embracing the values of discipline and fair play. As the event concluded, winners were awarded, but beyond the medals and trophies, the true victory lay in the unforgettable memories and strengthened bonds within the RNHS community.
