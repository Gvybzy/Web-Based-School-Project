Facilities

Rosales National High School is equipped with modern classrooms, comfortable seating, and up-to-date learning materials, alongside the shared facilities below.

Library

A sanctuary for intellectual exploration and academic growth, equipped with a diverse collection of books and resources to support research and learning.

School Gymnasium

An energetic hub dedicated to physical fitness and holistic well-being, hosting PE classes, sports training, and school-wide events like Intramurals.

Lounge Area

A charming space for relaxation and socializing, nestled amid greenery with comfortable benches — a tranquil escape from the school day.

RNHS

Rosales National High School (RNHS) is a public secondary institution in Rosales, Pangasinan, offering junior high school and the K to 12 Senior High School program under the Department of Education.
