Academic Track
(STEM)


Science, Technology, Engineering, and Mathematics are intertwining disciplines when applied in the real world. The difference of the STEM curriculum with the other strands and tracks is the focus on advanced concepts and topics.


Under the track, you can become a pilot, an architect, an astrophysicist, a biologist, a chemist, an engineer, a dentist, a nutritionist, a nurse, a doctor, and a lot more. Those who are also interested in Marine Engineering should take this track.

What is STEM Strand?


The STEM (Science, Technology, Engineering, and Mathematics) strand in our Academic Track is tailored for students passionate about science and technology. This program prepares them for future careers in fields such as engineering, computer science, biology, and physics.


The Science, Technology, Engineering, and Mathematics (STEM) strand is designed for students who have a passion for innovation, problem-solving, and scientific discovery. This strand focuses on developing critical thinking, analytical, and technical skills through subjects like physics, chemistry, biology, and advanced mathematics. STEM prepares students for careers in engineering, medicine, information technology, and other science-related fields by providing hands-on experiments, research opportunities, and real-world applications of scientific concepts. By taking this track, students gain a strong foundation in scientific inquiry and technological advancements, equipping them for higher education and future careers in rapidly evolving industries.
